Women Calendar

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Women Calendar is a menstrual cycle tracking application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Information Stored by the App

Women Calendar allows users to record menstrual cycle start and end dates. These dates are stored locally on the user's device using SharedPreferences.

The app also stores the selected language locally. Cycle day numbers are calculated from saved dates and are not separately stored.

3. Data Collection and Transmission

Women Calendar does not transmit menstrual cycle information to the developer or to an application backend server.

The application does not currently provide accounts, cloud synchronization, or server-side storage of calendar entries.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Calendar entries remain in the app's local storage until they are changed, deleted, or the application's local data is cleared.

Uninstalling the application normally removes its local data. However, device operating systems may provide backup and restoration functionality according to the user's device settings.

5. Data Sharing

Women Calendar does not share menstrual cycle entries with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties through its own backend.

6. Data Security

Menstrual cycle information is stored locally within the application's storage. Users should protect their devices with appropriate security measures, such as a screen lock.

7. Children's Privacy

Women Calendar is intended for users who want to track their menstrual cycles. The application is not specifically designed for children.

8. Your Choices

Users can manage their recorded cycle dates within the application. Local application data can also be cleared through the device settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
